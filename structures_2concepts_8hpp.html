Detailed Description

Contracts for discrete structures, stated as the algebra they maintain.

A union-find is a representation of an equivalence relation on \(\{0,\dots,N-1\}\) together with its quotient set. A graph is a finite incidence structure. Naming the structure rather than the method list is what lets the laws be stated, and num::structures::debug samples them.
