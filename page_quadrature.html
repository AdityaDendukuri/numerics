3. Gaussian Quadrature (num::gauss_legendre)

Exact for polynomials of degree up to \(2n - 1\). Supports \(n \in [1, 5]\) nodes.

5. Inverse Laplace Contour Integration (num::talbot_quadrature)

Evaluates the Bromwich contour integral \(f(t) = \mathcal{L}^{-1}[F](t) \approx \sum_{k=1}^M w_k F(z_k)\) along the Weideman–Talbot hyperbolic contour:

num::talbot_quadrature contour{/*n_nodes=*/16};
auto nodes = contour.nodes(/*t=*/1.0); // Precomputed complex nodes and weights
