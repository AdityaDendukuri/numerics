Detailed Description

scalar field traits shared by concepts and runtime invariant sampling.

Numerical structure is stated over a scalar field \(\mathbb{K}\) (typically \(\mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\)). These traits let structure and diagnostics be written once and hold for both, rather than being fixed to double.
