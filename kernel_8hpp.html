Detailed Description

Tier-0 umbrella: raw compute over pointers and callables.

SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT Part of numerics, (c) 2026 Aditya Dendukuri. https://github.com/AdityaDendukuri/numerics

Everything reachable from this header is templated on the scalar type, takes raw pointers and callables, allocates nothing, and includes nothing outside the standard library. No containers, no concepts, no algebra: the kernel computes and makes no claims about what it computes on.

That is what makes this tier copyable. A consuming project can vendor these files, or a single routine out of them, without adopting anything else.
