Detailed Description

Block LU factors of a block-tridiagonal matrix.

After factorization the matrix reads \(PA P^{T} = \mathcal{L}\,\mathcal{U}\) with \(\mathcal{L}\) block unit lower bidiagonal (its off-diagonal blocks in lower) and \(\mathcal{U}\) block upper bidiagonal (diagonal on the diagonal, upper off it).
