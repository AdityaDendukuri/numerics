Detailed Description

Krylov subspace matrix exponential-vector product: compute exp(t*A)*v.

Approximates \(\exp(tA)v \approx \|v\| Q_m \exp(tH_m)e_1\) where \(AQ_m \approx Q_{m+1}\bar{H}_m\) is the Arnoldi relation.
