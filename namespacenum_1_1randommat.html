◆ clique_sampler

enum class num::randommat::clique_sampler : std::uint8_t
strong

How the star-mesh clique left by each elimination is approximated.

Enumerator
exact 

Keep the full clique. Correct, and densifies.

independent 

Kyng-Sachdeva: samples independent draws per neighbour.

tree 

A random spanning tree of the clique, reweighted to stay unbiased.

Spanning-tree clique sampler: one reweighted random tree per elimination.

Keeps \(d-1\) edges per eliminated vertex instead of \(d\), drawn from a negatively dependent distribution rather than independently. See num::structures::sample_clique_tree for why the reweighting needs no effective-resistance solve.
