Detailed Description

High-performance resolvent solver (sI - A)^-1 b reusing a precomputed Hessenberg decomposition.

Preprocessing cost: O(n^3) once (Hessenberg reduction A = Q H Q^T). Per-shift solve cost: O(n^2) (Hessenberg-structured Gaussian elimination + Q back-projection).
