Assert that apply_adjoint really is the adjoint of apply.

Verified by \(\langle Ax, y \rangle = \langle x, A^* y \rangle\) over random probes. A transposed-but-unconjugated adjoint passes on real data and fails here on complex data, which is exactly where it would otherwise corrupt LSQR and the Arnoldi recurrence.
