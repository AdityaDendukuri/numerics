Detailed Description

template<std::floating_point T>
struct num::kernel::gemm_config< T >

The blocking gemm uses on this target, derived at compile time.

The structure is the Goto/BLIS one: a register-tiled microkernel computing an mr x nr block of C over kc inner products, fed from packed copies of A and B so that it streams contiguous, zero-padded panels rather than strided rows of the caller's matrices. Three loops around it block for the memory hierarchy: an mc x kc slab of A held in L2, a kc x nc panel of B held across one sweep of that slab, and the mr x kc / kc x nr slivers the microkernel touches held in L1.

Only five integers are target-specific, and every one is derived from the macros in kernel/vector.hpp:

NEON / SSE2 AVX2 AVX-512
mr x nr (double) 8 x 6 / 6 x 4 6 x 8 14 x 16

which are the shapes BLIS ships for those targets. kc, mc and nc come from NUM_K_L1_BYTES, NUM_K_L2_BYTES and NUM_K_GEMM_PANEL_BYTES.

Definition dense.hpp:153

Inner-product length per microkernel call. The kc x nr B sliver stays in L1 across the whole sweep of A tiles, so it gets half of L1; the A slivers stream from the L2-resident slab. Capped at 512: on a large L1 a longer kc only shrinks the other two blocks, measured here as a loss at n = 256..512 and no gain above.
