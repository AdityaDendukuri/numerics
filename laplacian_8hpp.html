Detailed Description

Matrices produced from a graph.

A graph in structures carries no algebraic operations. The adjacency matrix, the Laplacian, and the Markov generator are matrices, so they are produced here, where matrix storage already lives.

The unnormalized Laplacian of an undirected weighted graph is \(L = D - W\), symmetric positive semi-definite with \(L\mathbf{1} = 0\). It is singular by construction, so tag it assume_psd rather than assume_spd.
