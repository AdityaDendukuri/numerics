Detailed Description

Power iteration, inverse iteration, Rayleigh quotient iteration.

Every matvec/dot/axpy/norm call inside these iterations goes through the untagged num::matvec/num::dot/... entry points, which resolve to num::accel (the build's best available backend) automatically.
